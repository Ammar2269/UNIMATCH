The Method

Three principles,
held without exception.

Everything below is a constraint we accepted at the cost of covering fewer schools. It is the whole reason the numbers here can be trusted.

№ 01

Your budget is the first filter, not the last.

Conventional search asks for a subject, returns a ranked list, and leaves you to discover the price at the very end. We invert it. Tuition ceiling and monthly living costs come first, so nothing you see is out of reach by the time you fall in love with it.

Budget-led ranking
№ 02

Every figure traces back to the university.

Each verified programme card carries links to the source pages the numbers came from. If a tuition fee or deadline cannot be confirmed on an official page, it does not become a match — it stays out, or it is labelled uncertain and you decide.

Source-linked data
№ 03

Nothing is estimated.

No modelled averages, no filled-in gaps, no invented prices dressed up as research.

No guessed numbers
№ 04

Match ≠ ranking.

A score here means fit against the profile you entered. It is not a claim about a university's global standing.

Honest scoring
№ 05

Yours, and only yours.

Your profile, wishlist, and checklist are stored in this browser. No account required, nothing transmitted.

Match Finder scores are only ever calculated from programmes UniMatch has personally verified and source-linked — never estimated. The directory is unscored and unverified beyond name, country, and website. University fees, deadlines, admission rules, and visa requirements change constantly. Treat both as a discovery starting point, then confirm every important detail on the official university page before paying anything. “Best match” means best fit for the profile entered, not an official global ranking.
