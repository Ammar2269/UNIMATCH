The Method

Three principles,
held without exception.

Everything below is a constraint we accepted at the cost of covering fewer schools. It is the whole reason the numbers here can be trusted.

№ 01

Your budget is the first filter, not the last.

Conventional search asks for a subject, returns a ranked list, and leaves you to discover the price at the very end. We invert it. Tuition ceiling and monthly living costs come first, so nothing you see is out of reach by the time you fall in love with it.

Budget-led ranking
№ 02

A figure either traces back to the university, or it says so.

Source-linked programmes carry links to the official pages their numbers came from. The rest are bulk records we have not yet checked — those are marked unverified on the card, carry no source links, and are excluded entirely when you switch on strict mode.

Source-linked, or labelled
№ 03

Nothing is invented.

No modelled averages, no filled-in gaps, no prices made up to fill a column. Every number here came from somewhere, and the card tells you where.

No guessed numbers
№ 04

Match ≠ ranking.

A score here means fit against the profile you entered. It is not a claim about a university's global standing.

Honest scoring
№ 05

Yours, and only yours.

Your profile, wishlist, and checklist are stored in this browser and nothing is transmitted. The account the finder asks for lives in this browser too — there is no server to send it to.

The open directory

A raw, searchable index — not scored or ranked, just names and official sites.

What this is: an open worldwide university dataset — school name, country, and official website only. It does not include tuition, living costs, majors, or degree levels, so it cannot be filtered by budget. Use it to discover schools by country or name, then check figures directly on each site.

The programme database holds two kinds of record. Source-linked programmes carry links to the official pages their figures came from. Unverified records come from bulk datasets, are labelled unverified wherever they appear, carry no source links, and are excluded from results entirely when strict mode is on. The directory is unscored and unverified beyond name, country, and website. University fees, deadlines, admission rules, and visa requirements change constantly. Treat both as a discovery starting point, then confirm every important detail on the official university page before paying anything. “Best match” means best fit for the profile entered, not an official global ranking.
